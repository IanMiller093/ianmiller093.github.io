description

I'm a sophomore at the University of Pennsylvania majoring in electrical engineering. Last summer, I interned in Dr. Mathew Madhavacheril's particle cosmology lab, where I worked on building a component separation algorithm for Cosmic Microwave Background images, and developed my signal processing and statistics intuition. I'm a member of PennAiR's electrical team, where, in the past, I've worked on sensor implementation and integration, LoRa communication, and power design.

I also care deeply about helping younger students and engineers. I'm a Student Coordinator at Penn Reading Initiative, my work study job in which I teach K-4th graders how to read. I'm also the Head TA of the EE Team of Access Engineering, which teaches local Philly high schoolers about engineering.

I'm currently working on a buck converter design to step 5 V down to 3.3 V. More info is on my projects page!
